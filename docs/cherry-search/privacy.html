Privacy Policy for Cherry Search

Effective Date: February 28, 2026
Last Updated: February 28, 2026

Introduction

McNish Development ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Cherry Search mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

Our Commitment to Privacy

Cherry Search is designed with privacy as a core principle. We believe your search activity and saved items should remain private and under your control.

Information We Collect

Information We DO NOT Collect

We want to be clear about what we do not collect:

  • ❌ We do NOT collect personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • ❌ We do NOT collect location data
  • ❌ We do NOT collect your search history on our servers
  • ❌ We do NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • ❌ We do NOT share any data with third parties
  • ❌ We do NOT require account creation or login
  • ❌ We do NOT collect device identifiers for tracking purposes

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  1. Saved Searches
    • Search terms and filters you choose to save
    • Platform preferences (eBay, Depop, Vinted, Vestiaire)
    • Price range and condition filters
  2. My Picks
    • Listings you choose to save ("pick") for later reference
    • Item images and prices cached at the time of picking
  3. App Preferences
    • Currency display preference
    • eBay regional marketplace preference
    • Display settings (compact/full view)

All of this information is stored using AsyncStorage (React Native's local storage system) and never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to our servers or any third-party services.

Third-Party Marketplace APIs

Cherry Search displays listings from third-party marketplaces. When you perform a search, the App sends your search query directly to the marketplaces you have enabled (eBay, Depop, Vinted, Vestiaire Collective). This is solely to retrieve search results. The App does not log, store, or transmit your searches to McNish Development.

Each marketplace has its own privacy policy:

  • eBay Privacy Notice
  • Depop Privacy Policy
  • Vinted Privacy Policy
  • Vestiaire Collective Privacy Policy

How We Use Information

The locally stored data is used solely to:

  • Remember your saved searches between app sessions
  • Show you items you have previously picked/saved
  • Maintain your display and regional preferences
  • Provide a seamless user experience without requiring an account

Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage
  • No data is transmitted to McNish Development servers
  • Data remains on your device until you delete the App
  • You can clear all stored data by deleting and reinstalling the App

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services, including:

  • No analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase)
  • No advertising networks
  • No crash reporting services
  • No social media integrations

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age. Since we do not collect any personal information, there are no special considerations for children's use of the App.

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal information on our servers, traditional data rights (access, deletion, portability) do not apply to McNish Development. However:

  • You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the App
  • You have complete control over your device's local storage
