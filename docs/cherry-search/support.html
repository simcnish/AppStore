Frequently Asked Questions

What is Cherry Search?
Cherry Search lets you search across multiple secondhand fashion and lifestyle marketplaces — eBay, Depop, Vinted, and Vestiaire Collective — all at once from a single search. Compare prices, save searches, and build your wishlist without switching between apps.
Which marketplaces does Cherry Search support?
Cherry Search currently supports eBay (with regional options: UK, US, DE, FR, AU, CA, IT, ES), Depop, Vinted, and Vestiaire Collective. You can enable or disable any platform from the search settings.
Do I need accounts on these marketplaces?
No accounts are needed to browse and search. Cherry Search displays publicly available listings. To purchase an item, you'll be taken to the marketplace's own app or website where you can complete the transaction.
What are "My Picks"?
My Picks is your personal wishlist. Tap the cherry 🍒 icon on any listing to save it to your Picks tab. Picked items are stored locally on your device — no account required. You can view them any time in the Picks carousel at the top of the home screen.
What are Saved Searches?
Save any search configuration (keywords, filters, platforms) so you can re-run it with one tap. Your saved searches appear in a carousel at the top of the search screen. Tap any saved search to instantly reload those results.
How does currency conversion work?
Cherry Search can display all prices in your preferred currency, fetching live exchange rates. Set your preferred currency in Settings. This is a display convenience — all purchases are processed in the marketplace's native currency.
Does the app require an internet connection?
Yes, an internet connection is required to search and load listings. Your saved searches and picked items are stored locally and always accessible offline, but live search results require connectivity.
Is there a cost to use Cherry Search?
Cherry Search is free to download and use. There are no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no advertising.

Platform Guide

eBay

eBay supports multiple regional marketplaces. Select your preferred region (UK, US, DE, FR, AU, CA, IT, ES) in Settings → eBay Region. Results include both Buy It Now and auction listings. Tap any listing to view it on eBay and buy directly.

Depop

Depop specialises in secondhand fashion, vintage clothing, and streetwear. Results show price, condition, and seller location. Tap to view the full listing on Depop.

Vinted

Vinted is a peer-to-peer marketplace for preloved clothing and accessories. Note: Vinted results may occasionally be unavailable if their platform temporarily blocks automated requests — this is a known limitation.

Vestiaire Collective

Vestiaire Collective focuses on authenticated luxury and designer fashion. Results display the seller's asking price along with condition and authentication status.

Troubleshooting

No results appearing

  1. Check your internet connection
  2. Ensure at least one platform is enabled in Settings
  3. Try a more general search term
  4. Check that price filters aren't too restrictive
  5. Force-close and reopen the app, then search again

Vinted results missing

  1. Vinted occasionally blocks automated search requests — this is outside our control
  2. Try again after a few minutes
  3. Other platforms will continue to show results normally

Images not loading

  1. Check your internet connection speed
  2. Try pulling down to refresh
  3. Some marketplace images may be unavailable if the listing has expired

Picked items not showing

  1. Check you're on the Picks tab (cherry 🍒 icon in the tab bar)
  2. Picks are stored locally — they won't transfer between devices
  3. If you've recently reinstalled the app, local data will have been cleared
